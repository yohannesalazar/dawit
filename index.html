About us

A UK-based trading business

DAG Global Ltd is a UK-based trading business focused on connecting buyers and suppliers across international markets. We aim to make sourcing and commercial coordination more straightforward through clear communication and a practical understanding of each customer's requirements.

Our areas of activity include wholesale trading, product sourcing and sales agency services where agreed. Our focus is on building commercial connections between the United Kingdom, Ethiopia and other international markets, matching product requirements with suitable supply opportunities.

We work from clearly defined specifications, quantities and delivery expectations. Each enquiry is assessed individually, with product availability, pricing, documentation and responsibilities confirmed in writing before an order proceeds.

Our approach is based on professional relationships, transparent discussions and careful coordination throughout the trading process.

Our mission

To help businesses access suitable products and build dependable trading relationships across markets.

Our vision

To become a trusted trading link between UK suppliers and international business customers.
